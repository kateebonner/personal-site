The Schrödinger figure

Figure 1 · live on the home page

A free Gaussian wavepacket evolving under the time-dependent Schrödinger equation, evaluated from the closed-form solution every frame and drawn as a line through x, Re ψ, and Im ψ inside the surface of revolution r = |ψ|. The packet moves to the right and disperses; the fast components run ahead, so the spiral tightens at the front.

I first built this animation as a teaching tool at Georgetown. This version runs in the browser with Three.js and shares one clock with the sequence on the home page, so the time cursor, the readout, and the packet always agree. How the hero animation works covers the math, the parameters, and the rendering.
