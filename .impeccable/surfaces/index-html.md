## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is drawn as the pulse-sequence diagram quantum control people sketch, rendered as a mechanical drawing sheet: labeled channel lanes, one ruled time axis, hatched pulses placed in time, the state evolving above them, all inside a bordered sheet with a title block. It refuses the portfolio arrangement of name, tagline, avatar, and three cards, and it refuses tinted, rounded, shadowed component chrome of any kind.

OWN-WORLD: White paper; black ink only, in three line weights (thin 1px for construction, dimension, hatching, and center lines; medium 1.5px for visible edges, frames, and the label-column divider; thick 2.5px reserved for the sheet border). Drafting red is the single accent: the time cursor, the active pulse outline, and focus. Fills are 45-degree hatching, never tints; the active pulse cross-hatches. Every page sits inside a sheet border with zone numbers along the top and bottom and zone letters down the sides, and closes with a title block (title, sheet number, revision, date, scale, email, LinkedIn). Lettering is upright engineering lettering, Share Tech, for headlines, labels, nav, and the title block; Share Tech Mono for readouts, ticks, dimensions, and zone marks; Atkinson Hyperlegible for long prose. Three text sizes plus one display size, plus one sheet-mark size for zone letters and title-block keys. With content removed the page still reads as a drawing sheet with a sequence diagram on it.

STORY: The visitor recognizes a drawing sheet and the notation before reading a word, reads the headline as the label of the state lane, watches a real wavefunction move under a live dimension, believes both halves of the claim, and clicks About or the email.
