# Surface brief: Home (index.html)

Scope: the landing surface of katebonner.ai. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: a hiring manager or technical recruiter at a quantum industry company, arriving from LinkedIn or an email, deciding in under a minute how to categorize Kate. Job: categorize her correctly as a physicist who builds quantum control software, then reach contact. Action: email kate@katebonner.ai or open About. Proof: the live, correct free-particle Schrödinger solution rendered as the state lane; the confirmed headline; the sequence of sections drawn in the notation of the work. Constraints: light theme, no photo, no analytics, static HTML/CSS/JS, reduced-motion and no-WebGL fallbacks, every claim traceable to the résumé.

Memorable moment: the page is a control sequence. One clock drives a time cursor across the section lanes while the wavepacket in the state lane evolves and disperses; scrubbing the time axis scrubs both.

Unresolved: exact loop period and camera; whether lane blocks fire on hover or only under the cursor; note authoring once several notes exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is drawn as the pulse-sequence diagram quantum control people sketch: labeled channels stacked as lanes, one ruled time axis, shaped pulses placed in time, and the state evolving above them. It refuses the portfolio arrangement of name, tagline, avatar, and three cards, and it refuses the full-bleed hero with text floating over motion.

OWN-WORLD: White ground; near-black ink hairlines for lane rules, the label-column divider, the time axis, and the vertical time cursor. Two channel hues from the audience's own figures, matplotlib blue and orange, used as filled pulse envelopes with darker ink variants for text. Labels, nav, and the headline in Barlow, a technical grotesk; prose in STIX Two Text, the physics publishing serif; readouts and code in B612 Mono, an instrument face. Three type sizes plus one display size; rank by weight, case, and rules. With content removed the page still reads as a sequence diagram: a label column, ruled lanes, an axis with ticks, a cursor, two colored pulses.

STORY: The visitor recognizes the notation before reading a word, reads the headline as the label of the state, watches a real wavefunction move, believes both halves of the claim, and clicks About or the email in the label column.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440 wide): A 56px top bar with the wordmark "Kate Bonner" left and a mono readout "t = 0.00" with a pause control right. Below, a 300px label column divided from the lanes by a hairline. State lane, about 56vh tall: label cell holds the headline (Barlow SemiBold, 40px, three lines), the subline (STIX, 18px), and a contact line (B612 Mono, 13px: email and LinkedIn). Lane content is the 3D ribbon of x versus Re ψ versus Im ψ in blue over a translucent envelope, axes labeled, at full lane width. Three 72px lanes follow, labels "1 About", "2 Projects", "3 Notes", each holding one filled pulse block positioned at staggered times with a one-line teaser and an arrow. A 32px ruled time axis with ticks closes the lanes; the cursor line crosses the three lanes and lights each block as it passes. Primary action: the email in the label cell and the About block, first in time. Mobile (390): label cell first, ribbon at 16:10, lanes stacked with labels above full-width blocks, axis retained.
