About

I'm Kate Bonner. I work on the founding team at Harmoniqs, where we build quantum optimal control software, and I'm finishing a master's degree in Quantum Science & Technology at Columbia, expected December 2026.

At Harmoniqs I work on amicode, the research environment scientists use to design and run pulse optimizations: the Piccolo.jl solver integration, pulse and fidelity visualization, the formulation and experiment context, and connections to quantum hardware.

Before this I spent five years shipping software and products: enterprise network security deployments at Darktrace, a React Native fintech app at Comun, first-party data services at NBCUniversal, and API demonstrations for enterprise fintech clients at Coast. I studied physics at Georgetown, where I fabricated silver-silicon junctions in the lab and, earlier, built 3D animations of the time-dependent Schrödinger equation as a teaching tool. That animation is the origin of the figure on the home page.
